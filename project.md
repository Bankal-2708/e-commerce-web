# Oryanta — Fashion, Personalized for You 👗✨

A full-stack fashion e-commerce platform with AI-powered styling, digital wardrobe, and virtual try-on. Supports two user roles: **customers** (browse, style, shop) and **sellers** (upload and manage products).
